## Space & size

Leave clear space around the mark or logo equal to the mark's height.

Use the tiled mark from 16 px for icons & avatars, and the logo from 20 px high next to other content.

## What to avoid

- Change the colors of the mark or the logo.
- Stretch, rotate or crop it.
- Retype the wordmark in another font.
- Add shadows, outlines or other effects.
- Place it on a photo or a busy pattern.
